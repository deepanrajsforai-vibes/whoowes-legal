How do I split an expense?

Tap the + button, enter the amount and what it was for, choose who you're splitting with, and pick a split mode — evenly, by exact amount, by percentage, or by shares. Then save.

How do I settle up?

Open a friend or group and tap Settle to record a payment. If the other person has added a UPI ID to their profile, you can pay them directly from the app in one tap.

How do I add friends?

Go to the Friends tab and tap Invite. You can add people by name, email, phone number, or directly from your contacts.

A friend can't see expenses I added before they joined

Ask them to add and verify their phone number in Account → Add phone number. Verifying the number links any expenses that were created for them before they signed up, so your shared balance is correct.

How do I delete my account?

Open Account → Delete Account in the app, or email us at nebulacorp.support@icloud.com and we'll remove your data.

Is my data private?

Yes. See our Privacy Policy for exactly what we collect and how it's used, and our Terms of Service.
